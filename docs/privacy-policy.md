# Zaka Play Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Zaka Play ("we", "us") is a rewards app operated by Rangarirai Brendan Mutasa, Durban, KwaZulu-Natal, South Africa. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why, and your rights under the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA).

## 1. Information we collect
- **Account details:** first name, cellphone number and email address you give at sign-up.
- **Payout details:** the cellphone number, email address, or bank/eWallet details you enter when you cash out.
- **Reward activity:** offers you start and complete, coins earned, pending and spent, referral codes used, and cash-out history.
- **Device and technical data:** a user ID, device model, operating system, app version, IP address and approximate country. Our offer partners use these to confirm installs and prevent fraud.
- **Advertising ID:** when you open offers, our offer partners may use your device's advertising ID to track offer completion.

We do not collect your contacts, photos, precise location or SMS messages.

## 2. Why we use it
- To create and secure your account.
- To credit coins when an offer partner confirms you completed an offer.
- To pay out the rewards you request.
- To detect and prevent fraud, multiple accounts, VPN or emulator abuse.
- To provide support and send important service messages.
- To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.

We process your information on the basis of your consent, to perform our agreement with you, and for our legitimate interest in preventing fraud.

## 3. Who we share it with
- **Offer partners (offerwall networks):** they receive your Zaka Play user ID and device data so they can show offers and confirm completions. Each partner has its own privacy policy.
- **Payout providers:** airtime, voucher and payment providers receive only the details needed to deliver your reward.
- **Service providers:** cloud hosting and database services (such as Google Firebase) that store data on our behalf.
- **Authorities:** when the law requires it.

We never sell your personal information.

## 4. Storage and cross-border transfers
Your information is stored on secure cloud servers, which may be outside South Africa. We only use providers with adequate data protection, as POPIA requires.

## 5. How long we keep it
We keep account and reward records while your account is active and for up to 5 years after, for tax and fraud-prevention purposes. Then we delete or anonymise them.

## 6. Security
We use encryption in transit, access controls, and server-side checks so coins can only be credited by verified partner confirmations.

## 7. Your rights
Under POPIA you may ask to access, correct or delete your personal information, object to processing, or withdraw consent. You can delete your account at any time by emailing us. You may also complain to the Information Regulator (South Africa): inforeg.org.za, complaints.IR@inforegulator.org.za.

## 8. Age limit
Zaka Play is only for people aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If we learn we have, we delete it.

## 9. Changes
We may update this policy. We will notify you in the app about important changes.
